Today

This question normally starts an integration project. Eight systems, each holding part of the answer, each with its own idea of what identifies a person.

Nothing here was integrated. The eight domains compose the same published component for the national identifier, so the join is the identifier itself. There is no mapping table to maintain and no reconciliation step to schedule, which is why the answer takes {{ q.elapsed }} seconds rather than a quarter.

What this did not show until 4.4.0. The question a minister would rather ask, which businesses employ exposed people and what trade is at risk, could not be answered by the earlier demonstration data: employment records carried the employer as free text, so the chain broke between Employment and Business Registry. The 4.4.0 models compose the Business Registry Number component in employment, tax and maritime records as well as the registry itself, and the second question below is the chain running.
